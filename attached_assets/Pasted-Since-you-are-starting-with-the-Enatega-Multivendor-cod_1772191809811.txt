3 Specific Changes You Must Make Immediately
Since Enatega is built for food, there are three "traps" you need to fix in the code:

1. The "Preparation Time" Logic
Food: Takes 20 mins to cook.

Medicine: Takes 2 mins to pick from a shelf.

The Change: In the enatega-multivendor-admin, you must reduce the default "Buffer Time" and "Preparation Time" to near-zero. Your goal is the 2-minute dispatch.

2. Item Variations
Food: "Extra Cheese" or "No Onions."

Medicine: "Strip of 10," "Half Strip," or "Bottle."

The Change: Refactor the Options and Add-ons logic in the Enatega schema to handle Dosage Units (mg/ml) and Packaging Units instead of food toppings.

3. Safety First (The "Kill Switch")
The Change: Add a "Restricted Item" logic. In the enatega-multivendor-app, ensure that Schedule X drugs (heavy narcotics) are either hidden or strictly flagged for "In-Person ID Verification" upon delivery.
